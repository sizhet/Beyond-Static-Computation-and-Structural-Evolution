## Overview

This initial release introduces a research synthesis connecting five complementary Structural Intelligence (SI) programs—Adversarial Structural Security Intelligence (ASSI), Dynamic Bucket Tree of Permutation (D-BTP), Constrained Delta Evolution (CDE), World Entity and Structural Intelligence Operations (WE-SIO), and Object-Centric Structural Reasoning and Evolution (OCSRE).

The central proposition is that computational systems may benefit from treating persistent objects, unresolved residuals, constrained state changes, discovery processes, and transferable capabilities as explicitly governed structures. Rather than replacing existing databases, optimizers, or learning algorithms, the program asks when these structures add measurable value beyond well-designed conventional and adaptive baselines.

## Research contributions

1. **Five-DOI convergence.** A shared conceptual map of security, directional hypothesis generation, constrained evolution, world-entity representation, and object-centric reasoning.
2. **Object-native intelligence.** A WE-SIO Core Frame with extensible plugins for identity, state, relations, history, residuals, and capabilities; including structural, semantic, and operational alignment with SQL systems.
3. **Structural evolution computing.** Explicit state containers, candidate deltas, constraints, path memory, evaluation, and potentially evolving candidate generators, with attention to established optimization and control methods.
4. **Residual-first, in-situ intelligence.** Persistent, provenance-bearing records of prediction errors, constraint conflicts, explanatory gaps, and unexploited opportunities that can guide further discovery and learning.
5. **Persistent structural discovery.** A resource-aware loop connecting adaptive observation, opportunity localization, CCC and Two-Way CCC, pattern triggering, sandbox testing, and independent validation; including the proposed Structural Emergence Fishing Laboratory (SEFL).
6. **Evidence-bound intelligence relay.** Transfer of capabilities together with contracts, provenance, evaluation evidence, limits, negative results, and unresolved questions, supporting Collective Learning without prematurely promoting hypotheses to facts.

## Validation commitments

The release presents **testable hypotheses and experimental protocols, not established performance results**. Evaluation should include:

- Comparable conventional, incremental, adaptive, and ablated baselines under matched computational budgets.
- Contract conformance and provenance checks before runtime performance claims.
- Held-out datasets, temporal integrity, independent replication, and appropriate uncertainty reporting.
- Explicit accounting for computational, maintenance, operational, and domain-specific costs.
- Publication of null results, failed transfers, unresolved residuals, and adverse security findings when responsibly disclosable.
- Evidence-gated promotion of candidate findings into validated capabilities and relay packages.

No general superiority over existing database systems, optimization methods, machine learning, or financial strategies is asserted by this release.
